The packaged encoder README identifies libavif v1.0.1 (BSD-2-Clause).
libavif links libaom (BSD-2-Clause and Alliance for Open Media Patent License 1.0).
libavif's licence inventory also contains third-party component notices; retain the entire inventory.
No claim is made that these licences resolve third-party patent claims. The owner selects deployment.
Only the single-threaded encoder and its WASM are used; decoder/MT assets are not used.
Strict CSP: omitting wasm-unsafe-eval can prevent WebAssembly.compile. The worker reports that as
unavailable and the format selector disables AVIF. No automatic format substitution is offered.
